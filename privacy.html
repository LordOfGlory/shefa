Privacy Policy

Effective 23 August 2026

Shefa Venturez respects the privacy of clients, students and visitors. This policy describes the information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you contact us, request a consultation or apply for a academy, we collect the details you provide. That typically includes name, organisation, email address, telephone or WhatsApp number, country, and information about your project or learning goals.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to enquiries, assess suitability for a service or academy, communicate about enrolment or delivery, and improve our operations. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

We may share information with service providers who help us operate communications, payments or hosting, and where we are required to do so by law. Those parties may process data only for the purpose we specify.

Retention and your rights

We keep information for as long as needed for the purpose collected or as required by law. You may request access, correction or deletion of information we hold by writing to shefaventurez@outlook.com.

Security

We apply appropriate administrative and technical measures to protect personal information. No method of transmission is completely secure; please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive data through public forms.
