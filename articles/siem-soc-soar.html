SIEM, SOC and SOAR without the acronym fog

SIEM collects and correlates events. A SOC is the human and process layer that watches and decides. SOAR encodes repeatable response so people are not typing the same steps forever. EDR and XDR add detection closer to endpoints and across more telemetry.

Tools without an operating model become noise. Start with questions: what must we see, who responds, and what is in scope?
