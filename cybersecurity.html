How an engagement works

No simulated dashboards and no unverified claims. Work is scoped in writing before testing begins.

1. Authorization

We confirm the client, assets, environment and written permission. Testing does not start without that agreement.

2. Assessment

Depending on scope: vulnerability review, authorized penetration testing, control audit, or architecture consulting.

3. Report and next steps

Findings are prioritized with practical remediation guidance. Monitoring and response work is planned only when requested.
